OCR-A LEIP — fonts/leip-ocra.woff2
===================================

An ORIGINAL digitization of the OCR-A character model (the typeface
defined by ANSI X3.17-1977 / ISO 1073-1:1976), authored for the LEIP
"Best Week of Charter" game on 6 August 2026. The glyph outlines are
generated from scratch by tools/build_ocra_font.py in this repository;
that script is the complete source of the font program.

WHY AN ORIGINAL DIGITIZATION (license verification record)
----------------------------------------------------------
The visual direction requires embedding a *freely-licensed* OCR-A
digitization and verifying the license of the embedded file. That
verification was carried out on 6 August 2026 and FAILED for the
commonly available "free" OCR-A files:

- The widely redistributed free OCR-A (CTAN fonts/ocr-a lineage:
  Tor Lillqvist 1985 -> Richard B. Wales 1988 -> Matthew Skala 2011-12,
  including the OCRA.otf/OCRA7.ttf copies bundled by various projects)
  carries Richard B. Wales' notice that the font "may be used freely,
  but cannot be distributed for profit". Matthew Skala's own package
  documentation (ocr.pdf, v0.2, 27 September 2012) confirms this and
  adds "I make no copyright claim on it myself" — so the Wales
  restriction flows through every descendant of that lineage.
  A no-profit distribution restriction is not a free license.

- Monotype's "OCR A Extended" (bundled with Windows/Office) is
  commercially licensed and cannot be redistributed at all.

Typeface *designs* (glyph shapes) are not subject to copyright in the
relevant jurisdictions; font *programs* are. OCR-A LEIP is therefore a
fresh, independent font program implementing the public-standard OCR-A
character shapes, covering only the glyph set the game's data voice
needs (digits, capitals, and chart punctuation; lowercase is mapped to
the capitals). It does not copy, trace, or convert any existing font
program.
